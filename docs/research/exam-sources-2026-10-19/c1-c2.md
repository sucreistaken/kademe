## 2. Patterns worth adopting (own wording)

**C1 versus C2 items**
- C1 MC tests a clear paraphrase of one passage statement; distractors change one relation (cause/effect, who, how much). C2 options are length-balanced and abstract, and distractors use absolute quantifiers or a wrong scope ("always", "only when", "first").
- C2 listening forces country-by-country or speaker-by-speaker attribution (who does what, where), so skimming for a keyword fails.
- C1 headline matching uses ten same-domain headlines; the difference is scope (international vs national vs one project), so only gist works.
- Insertion pieces: the unused C2 pieces are on-topic and plausible (a meta-study, a basic-emotions paragraph), not contradictory. Linking cues are anaphora ("this approach"), concessive turns, quote continuation, not repeated nouns.
- C1 open cloze: roughly half the 15 gaps are fixed phrases or verb collocations ("sich zur Wehr setzen"-type), the rest structure words (relative pronoun, comparison, passive agent). It is lexicon in context, not a rule drill.
- C2 proof-reading error types: spelling, article/adjective ending, singular/plural verb agreement, preposition choice, prefix-verb choice, capitalised polite pronoun, missing linking letter in a compound.
- Note-completion gaps need a noun form, an abstract adjective, or a synonym; the key accepts meaning, not one fixed word.

**Text types and topics**: science and psychology (attachment, emotion, time perception), language acquisition and multilingualism, media, nutrition, nature and species, education, family policy, plus promo and press texts. Work topics are only one slice.

**Writing and speaking**: every prompt names a communicative goal plus a register (turn curt notes into polite text; defend against objections; no audience address in written form). Data-based tasks combine summary, interpretation, "how is it in your country", personal view. The own-country comparison is a fixed bullet in all three C1 Schreiben 2 topics and in the C1 Sprechen 3 talk.

## 4. Top 10 recommendations (ranked)

1. **Text-level open cloze (C1 and C2), GAP_FILL with typed gaps.** 12-15 gaps, `answers` listing every acceptable word, half collocations/fixed phrases, no choices. Original gap: "Die Stadt hat dem Antrag ___ Bedenken zugestimmt." (trotz/ungeachtet(+Gen), accept per key).
2. **Note-completion for lectures (C1 and C2), GAP_FILL.** Notes with 8-10 gaps, answers as synonym lists, one lecture script per level (reuse existing scripts after the rewrite in #8). Gap: "Das Phänomen verstärkt sich vor allem bei ___ Feuchtigkeit." (hoher/erhöhter/starker).
3. **Reading MC hygiene.** Rule: correct option is the longest in at most ~30% of items; equalise length; replace negation distractors by scope shifts and quantifier traps. Rework c1.ts:511, 528, 544, 574, 582, 618, 862.
4. **Diversify topics.** At least 5 of 12 stimuli per level outside work/economy: acoustics, sleep research, urban history, bilingual education, nutrition labels, museum policy. Keeps the exam from rewarding workplace vocabulary only.
5. **Recalibrate grammar.** Move the B2 items listed in section 3.4 down to B2 (or replace with genuine C1/C2 forms), fix absurd distractors (3.5), and turn c2.ts:999 into an auto-scored SHORT_TEXT (`accepted` list) next to the free-text version. Original C2 stem: "Man behauptet, sie habe es gewusst. (soll)" -> "Sie soll es gewusst haben."
6. **Better insertion and headline matching (MATCHING).** 5 gaps, 7 pieces, extras on-topic and cohesive with several gaps; cues via pronouns and connectors, not repeated nouns. Add C1 headline matching: 5 short texts, 8 same-field headlines differing in scope.
7. **Proof-reading and correct-the-detail tasks (GAP_FILL).** C2 proof-reading: show a 15-line text with 8 numbered words, type the correct form for each (spelling, case, agreement, preposition, prefix verb). Listening: an announcement table with 5 wrong details; learner types the right one. Original error: "Das Museum hat wegen des Umbaus geschlossen worden." (wurde/ist ... geschlossen).
8. **Make scripts sound spoken and longer.** Aim for 600-800 words, add repair ("also, besser gesagt"), asides, discourse markers, a questioning turn; remove the narrator prose in the C2 feature; keep one tough speaker attribution task per script.
9. **Source-based writing.** (a) C1 annotated-reply: formal complaint e-mail plus the learner's shorthand notes, goal "get the request approved despite your own mistake". (b) C1 data task: a 6-line text "survey" in the prompt, summarise, interpret, compare with your country, own view, 250 words. (c) C2 fact sheet to promo text with headline and lead.
10. **Speaking from handouts and role conflicts.** C1: replace one-sentence inputs by a 3-source handout (two survey lines, one study line, one article extract) with summary/opinion/own-country/own-behaviour bullets. C2: phone problem-solving with a stated rule to uphold, and a "defend your position" turn with 3 supplied counter-arguments. Example bullet: "Erklären Sie, warum die Frist nicht verlängert werden kann, und bieten Sie eine Ersatzleistung an."
