## 2. Item-writing patterns worth adopting

**Sprachbausteine Teil 1 (maps to GRAMMAR).** The three options are three forms of the same word (person/case/ending), never different words. Typical gap mix per 10: ~3 verb forms (Präteritum/modal, Konjunktiv II, auxiliary or passive), ~3 pronoun/article/ending forms, ~2 connectors, 1 preposition, 1 infinitive form. Decisive clue is always in the sentence: verb position after a connector, the subject earlier, the noun's gender elsewhere. Patterns seen across tests 1-3 (keys checked for tests 1 and 2):
- connector vs adverb at the same slot, decided by verb-final order (nebensatz vs hauptsatz);
- relative pronoun where the case comes from the verb inside the clause;
- formal-letter trap: capitalised possessive "Ihrer" vs lower-case "ihrer";
- genitive article after "wegen"; plural form after a numeral (Monat/Monate/Monaten);
- infinitive family after a modal (bare vs participle vs zu-form);
- haben/sein/werden auxiliary choice; reflexive pronoun case; person agreement of "werden";
- predicative vs attributive vs comparative adjective; "oder" vs "sonst" for a warning.
All options are real words; none is a non-word.

**Sprachbausteine Teil 2.** A short ad sets the facts, then a letter answers it, so every gap is constrained by the ad's content plus grammar. The box holds mostly function words (prepositions, connectors, particles, modal forms) and 1-3 content words. The 5 unused words are near-fits: a connector that fits the meaning but not the word order (weil vs deshalb), a preposition with the wrong government (für vs über), a modal form pair (würden vs müssten), degree words (sehr/viel/mehr/noch). Because each word is used once, elimination is part of the skill.

**How difficulty is created.** Not by rare words but by (a) keeping the clue 1-2 clauses away, (b) near-identical option pairs, (c) text register switches (du-letter vs Sie-letter). Distractors in reading echo words from the text but attach them to the wrong fact.

**Reading.** Text types: blog, press, ads, rules, reader comments, headings. R/F only at B1 (no "not in text"); ZB1 T4 uses opinion stance (Ja/Nein). Ad matching always has a "none" key and two ads that look right but break one detail (day, price, audience).

**Listening style.** Short texts are functional (voicemail, traffic, station, shop ad) at roughly 40-90 words; vox-pop has 5 voices, each stating a general view then a personal exception; interviews are interviewer + expert with statements in script order. The key is hidden by mentioning both values (old and new time, general belief and own habit) and by false statements that flip one detail or negate it. R/F keys in telc tests are mixed (2-3 true of 5; 4-6 true of 10), not patterned.

**Writing.** Each Leitpunkt names a different speech act (report, accept/decline, explain a custom, give a preference, ask a question). The task supplies an authentic stimulus whose own questions must be answered. Advice to examinees: ~2 sentences per point, do not merge two points in one sentence, vary sentence openings, link with weil/dass/ob/wenn. Length is calibrated to points (3 points = 80 words; 4 points = up to 150).

**Speaking.** Planning bullets end with an open "..." (invites own ideas); presentations are 5 labelled slides; the info-gap chart task (each partner has a different graphic) forces real exchange.

2. **Correct option is the longest in 6 of 10 reading and 5 of 9 listening choice items** (script check), e.g. 569 (key covers two ideas, distractors one each) and 862.
3. **Absurd distractors.** 588-589 (company forces everyone to cycle; buses stopped), 862 (bakery "with only one Filiale" contradicts the previous sentence), word-bank pools 334, 349, 364 (Bahnhof, Suppe, teuer, geschlafen, fahren) fit no gap by topic or word class; telc distractors are same-class near-fits.

5. **Level mismatch.** HARD items at 201 (seit, A2) and 205 (bevor vs nachdem, A2), EASY 134 and 138 (zu; werden given in brackets) are A2 tasks. HARD items at 189-198 (ohne zu, worden, was) are genuinely B1.
6. **Missing grammar families** that telc tests every time: Perfekt auxiliary, reflexive/personal pronoun case, comparative/predicative adjective, verb+preposition (only in cloze notes 270-320), wegen+genitive article, plural after numerals, V2 vs verb-final connector as a standalone item.
7. **Topic monoculture.** All 3 listening clips and 4 of 4 reading texts are work/training (446-540, 760-797); writing and speaking are also 8 of 12 job-related. Source exams spread across health, media, environment, travel, housing. Sub-scores will partly measure job vocabulary [may be intended for a hiring product].

Only one voicemail covers the short-text type.
9. **Listening scripts are clean prose.** Clip 3 (797+) has full sentences with almost no fillers or repair; clip 2 is one 269-word monologue read as a written speech. The key sentence is often a near-verbatim quote ("halb neun", "motiviert") [judgement].

11. **Writing:** 4 points in 70-110 words (1022, 958) is tight (telc gives up to 150 for 4); no prompt supplies a letter with questions to answer; two prompts clone the ZB1 A1/A3 speech-act pattern (965, 1003).

## 4. Top 10 recommendations

1. **Randomise TFNG order and NG count** (0-2 NG per item; keep R/F for B1 at least half the time). Cheapest, biggest integrity fix (item 1 above).
2. **Add a telc-style function-word bank** (new wordBank text: a reply letter to a short ad, ~10 gaps, 5 near-fit distractors such as a connector with the wrong word order). Example: gap "Wir kommen gern, ___ (damit/deshalb/weil) der Termin passt" with weil in the pool where only deshalb fits.
3. **Replace non-word and A2 distractors** (150, 112, 201, 205); use real near-forms (konnte/konntest/konntet pattern).
4. **Add GAP_FILL "Sprachbausteine 1" texts** with 10 form-contrast gaps in a letter, using the missing families in weakness 6.
5. **Add 3-4 short listening clips (40-90 words)**: traffic, station, shop, answerphone; one Q each (SINGLE_CHOICE), distractor mentioned in the script.
6. **Add a "wer sagt was" listening** using MATCHING: left = 5 opinions, right = 3 speakers (moderator + 2 guests); script 3 voices if TTS allows, else interview.
7. **Add Ja/Nein opinion matching** (reading): 6 short reader comments about a rule, MATCHING left = comments, right = "dafür / dagegen".
8. **Add one 350-450 word press text** (SINGLE_CHOICE 3-sentence best-match items) and cut work-only topics to ~50% (health, media, travel, housing, environment).
9. **Rewrite correct-option length** so the key is not the longest and every distractor reuses a real detail of the text (change 569, 588-589, 862).
10. **Writing/speaking:** add a prompt that embeds a received letter with 2 questions and 4 Leitpunkte (max 150 words); add a "Statistik" speaking prompt that gives a small table in the prompt text (image support unverified).
    Example Leitpunkte: answer the host's question about food, accept the date, describe your city's custom, ask one question back.
