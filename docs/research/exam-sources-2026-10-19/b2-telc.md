# B2 findings: telc sources vs. Kademe bank

Sources read: telc B2 Uebungstest 1 (2011), telc B2 tips booklet, telc B2+ Beruf Uebungstest 1 (2012), Zertifikat Deutsch fuer den Beruf (ZDfB) Modelltest 1 (2007), plus extras: Mit Erfolg Modelltests 1-3 (OCR, noisy), the Klett prep book, telc B2-C1 Medizin Uebungstest 1. All descriptions are my own words. Bank file: src/db/seed-bank/levels/b2.ts ("b2.ts").

## 1. Source inventory

**telc Deutsch B2 (general), 300 pts, 225 written + 75 oral, pass = 60 % in each half.**
- Reading+Sprachbausteine, 90 min together (about 10 min of it for transferring answers; all texts about 3000 words). Reading R1 global: 5 texts, 10 headings (a-j), 750-900 words, 5 pts each. R2 detail: one popular-science text 500-600 words, 5 three-option MC, items follow text order, 5 pts each. R3 selective: 10 situations, 12 info texts, 960-1200 words, spare "x" (no text fits), 2.5 pts each. Reading = 75 pts, 25 %.
- Sprachbausteine = 30 pts, 10 %. T1: a letter/email, about 250 words, 10 gaps, three options a/b/c, 1.5 pts/gap. T2: a magazine text, 300-350 words, 10 gaps, a 15-word box (a-o), 5 distractors, 1.5 pts/gap.
- Listening, about 20 min, played ONCE, all true/false (+/-): T1 news, 5 items, 30 s reading time; T2 radio interview, 10 items, 60 s; T3 five short announcements, 5 items. 75 pts.
- Writing, 30 min, choose 1 of 2 letters (information request / complaint), each built on an advert, min. 150 words, bullet points: cover 3 of 4, or 2 plus one own aspect. Criteria: task coverage, communicative design, formal correctness; x3 = 45 pts. Gate: if criterion I or III is D, the whole text scores 0.
- Speaking, 20 min preparation, 15 min exam, pairs/trios, 75 pts. Warm-up (unscored), T1 presentation of a chosen topic (90 s) plus questions, T2 discussion of a short controversial newspaper text, T3 joint problem solving (plan a trip for a described group; only the situation is given).

**telc B2+ Beruf, 100 pts, 75 written + 25 oral.** Reading 60 min: 20 MC across 3-6 authentic texts (business email, training programme, HR notice, instructions with right/wrong items, choose the best subject line). Writing 60 min: T1 formal letter 180-200 words that answers an incoming letter with the manager's marked passages, six mandatory points (all six = full marks); T2 email/note 60-80 words with margin notes. Listening about 30 min: T1 six R/F news, T2 seven MC (lecture, product presentation, interview), T3 seven MC from a 4-6 person meeting, one item per speaker. Sprachbausteine 30 min: T1 (new format) error check: an underlined word is either right (a) or replaced by b/c; text is a business letter (Kuendigung). T2 AGB-style text with a 15-word box. Speaking 17-18 min: T1 prepared presentation of own product/job/department, T2 discussion of a newspaper text with examples "from your company", T3 negotiation role-play (office move, supplier delay by phone) with opposed roles A/B.

**ZDfB Modelltest 1 (2007), 200 pts (150 + 50).** Structures and vocabulary 30 min, 40 items, three subtests: vocabulary cloze (15 MC, 3 options, near-synonym distractors), grammar cloze (15 MC, 3 options), proofreading (10 lines, at most one error per line, write the correct form or tick). Reading 40 min (gist/selective/total). Listening 30 min: 10 items of phone messages as note forms (fill in names, numbers, reasons), 5 MC conversation, 10 radio items. Correspondence 60 min: letter from text modules (15 pts) and letter from keywords (25 pts, 100-120 words). Oral (individual): self-presentation without notes, describe and comment a chart, business case role-play.

**Mit Erfolg models / Medizin.** Same format. Medizin (B2-C1) adds listening with 4 parts (two dialogues, lecture, discussion), a Sprachbausteine T1 as a doctor-patient dialogue with a word box containing multi-word chunks and discourse particles, T2 a clinical report with specialist lexis, writing = completing an Arztbrief, oral = patient interview, case presentation to colleagues, talking to relatives. For hiring it shows that a profession-specific exam tests register and genre in the real workplace role, not general topics.

## 2. Item-writing patterns worth adopting

**Sprachbausteine T1 (MC, 3 options).** From the tips booklet and the four tests: the tested points are all word classes, tied to context: case of article/adjective after a preposition; relative pronouns incl. genitive and preposition+relative; da-compounds (darauf/daran/dazu/darueber) selected by the verb; reflexive pronoun case; subjunctions (ob, dass, wenn, falls, als) and two-part connectors; Konjunktiv II past ("without you ... would have"); verb word order after "und dann"; participle adjectives (-end/-t); prefix verbs sharing one stem (families like ueber- verbs), modal particles/adverbs, bis vs bis zu. Distractors are built from ONE family per gap (case endings; or same-prefix verbs; or pronoun series), never nonsense. Gap density about one per 25 words; sentence text is natural correspondence; gaps often sit next to each other (two-part constructions); the first sentences are gap-free; some gaps need the whole sentence, so the book advises reading to the sentence end. Pool size: 10 gaps per text.

**Sprachbausteine T2 (box).** Pool of 15 for 10 gaps. The words are given in the exact form that fits (infinitive for plural-finite slots, no inflection needed), so it tests lexis and connection, not morphology. Mix: prepositions (an, auf, von, in), connectors (also denn/weil/darum/damit/dass in one pool), da-compounds, modals (three similar ones as decoys), verbs, adjectives, adverbs (zwar, doch, indessen). Decoys come from the same classes as answers and fit syntactically somewhere but not semantically. Density about one gap per 30 words.

**Beruf T1 error check.** Each gap shows the base form; the candidate decides whether it is already right or picks the corrected form. Tests agreement, verb form, case, spelling of derivations in a business letter. Good transfer to our MC cloze (keep the original word as one option).

**ZDfB proofreading.** One error per line, correct form typed. Maps to GAP_FILL typed answer or C-test style.

**Reading.** Global: five short texts versus ten similar headings (decoys share vocabulary with the text). Detail: items follow text order, each option fails by one wrong detail or a negation; do not test world knowledge. Selective: situations are short and concrete ("a friend wants ..."), texts are programmes/ads, one situation has no match. Beruf adds: business email with unknown outcomes, programme eligibility ("you could attend courses 1 and 4"), right/wrong statements against instructions, best subject line for a text.

**Listening script style.** Radio news = several unrelated short reports; interview with weather/place expert; short announcements (service hotline, ticket sale, tariff, event). Statements paraphrase and often invert one detail; no NG. Beruf meeting: each speaker takes a position (reports, proposes, objects, agrees and adds, warns, decides), and the item asks what each speaker's role/stance is. ZDfB phone messages: caller, company, reason, number, requested action.

**Writing.** Telc sets a realistic stimulus (advert) and a role; the points are speech acts (ask, describe own situation, list wishes). Complaint points: expectation after the advert, what happened, what you demand, what you do otherwise. Beruf adds a reply letter with six fixed points and an internal reply email with notes.

**Speaking.** Presentation = a chosen experience with 3-4 cue words; discussion = a one-page article with a claim, then pros/cons from own experience; joint planning = a bare situation, partners propose and react. Beruf negotiation gives each partner opposed interests and asks for a compromise. ZDfB: describe and comment a chart.

## 3. Bank audit (b2.ts, verified by reading)

**Counts.** GRAMMAR: 24 single items (lines 105-222; 18 SINGLE_CHOICE, 6 GAP_FILL; 9 EASY/9 MID/6 HARD), 3 C-tests (224, 245, 263; gap counts [unverified]), 3 MC clozes with 33 gaps (285, 302, 318), 3 word banks with 24 gaps (335, 350, 365). Skill tags: passiv, zustandspassiv, passiversatz x3-4, konjunktiv1 x2, konjunktiv2 x3, partizipattribut x2, nominalisierung x3, funktionsverbgefuege x2, konnektoren x4, genitivpraepositionen x2, praepositionen 1, modalpartikeln 1. READING: 4 texts (about 1,200 words total vs about 3,000 in telc), 16 items: 9 SINGLE_CHOICE, 3 MATCHING (13 rows), 4 TRUE_FALSE_NG (14 statements); tags detail 7, gist 3, inference 3, vocabulary 2, structure 1. LISTENING: 3 clips (about 240-450 words, 1-2 speakers), 12 items: 9 SINGLE_CHOICE, 3 TRUE_FALSE_NG; detail 9, gist 1, attitude 1, inference 1. WRITING: 6 prompts (3 forum opinion, 3 email/message; one complaint, line 1055). SPEAKING: 6 prompts (2 presentation, 2 discussion, 1 narration, 1 voice message). No SHORT_TEXT, MULTI_CHOICE items at B2.

**Vs. blueprint.** telc has 20 R/F listening items, 10+ detail reading items per text and 20 Sprachbausteine gaps; we have comparable gap totals (57 plus C-tests) but a much lighter reading load, no news/announcement/phone listening, no letter-with-stimulus writing, no joint-planning speaking, no "no match" selective reading, no error-check cloze.

**Weaknesses (each read at the cited line).**
1. Cloze g1 (line 289) and g5/g9 (293, 296): three of four distractors are pure subject-verb agreement slips; any candidate who sees the subject solves them. Same in 2nd text g9 (313: article "der" before the gap excludes every verb form) and g11 (314: "hat" after the gap excludes the non-participles, "gelohnen" is a non-word); 3rd text g2 (323: "gestiegt" is a non-word) and g6/g8 (327, 329) test the identical "Partizip II + werden" slot twice.
2. Gap density: 1 gap per 12-13 words in the clozes (computed: 143, 135, 149 words for 11 gaps), 1 per 16-18 in the word banks, against telc's 1 per 25-30. Gaps crowd each other, so context is thin, and our 4-option format and the density together make the clozes feel like a drill rather than a text.
3. Word banks (335-379) hold only content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives); telc pools mix prepositions, connectors, da-compounds. Many decoys are excluded by gender/ending alone (e.g. line 348 "Werkstatt" cannot follow "kleines"; line 378 "Verkehr" cannot follow "dauerhafte").
4. Line 133: drop-down offers {als, wie, desto} but the key list also accepts "umso" (not offered). The same point (je ... desto) returns in the third cloze g4 (line 325): duplicated skill.
5. Line 119-122 vs 193-196: both test "Konjunktiv II past, hatte vs. waere"; line 196 "gekommen wuerde" is ungrammatical, so one distractor is a giveaway. The item at 193 is MID but sits under the HARD banner; 210 (EASY) too.
6. Lines 171-173: "ausserhalb vs innerhalb" decided by the words "im Garten"; tests everyday meaning, not a B2 grammar point. Line 218-221 (HARD): "Wert legen auf" is a B1 pattern. Lines 123-126 and 197-200 ask metalinguistic questions ("which form is Konjunktiv I") instead of using the form in context.
7. Line 127-130: key "zur Verfuegung gestellt"; explanation says "zur Verfuegung geben" does not exist. I did not verify that against a dictionary [unverified]; it is a key-ambiguity risk, so add "geben" only after checking, or replace with a safer pair.
8. Listening: no clip has 3+ speakers, no news, no announcement, no voice message; all statements use NG (telc has none). Reading: the MATCHING items have spare options only on the right side (lines 461, 573, 637), so no "no text fits" row, although the app already supports a "none" target (see src/lib/exam/safe.test.ts around lines 130-160).
9. Workplace content: only one workplace text (coworking rules, line 611); no email, schedule, job advert, order/delivery, HR notice. Writing prompts have no stimulus at all (line 1077: stimuli empty), so there is no "answer this advert/email" task.
10. Answer position: in 18 grammar, 9 reading and 9 listening single-choice items the key is never option d (verified by listing the key digits: only 0, 1, 2). The exam shuffles options when serving (safe.ts / safe.test.ts:120-123), so candidates do not see it; it is only a seed hygiene issue.

## 4. Top 10 recommendations (existing item types only)

1. **Rebuild the cloze set to telc density:** 200-250 word letter/email texts, 10 gaps, about 25 words apart, include adjacent paired gaps. Keep 4 options but make every distractor a real form of the same family (case series, pronoun series, same-stem verbs). Ban non-words and pure agreement decoys. Use MC cloze (cloze.ts).
2. **Add Beruf-style error-check cloze:** reuse cloze() with the original word as one option. Example: "Wir bitten Sie, den Vertrag bis Freitag [[zu unterschreiben|unterschreiben|unterschrieben|unterschreibt]]." (answer: zu-infinitive after bitten), in a Mahnung/Lieferverzug letter.
3. **Word bank with function words:** pool 15 for 10 gaps, mixing prepositions, connectors, da-compounds, modals; decoys from the same families. Example pool excerpt: answers "zudem", "daran", "sofern"; decoys "dennoch", "darum", "falls" only if each is clearly wrong in its gap (check by blind solve, as the builder already requires).
4. **Close grammar-inventory gaps** seen in telc but absent from b2.ts (checked by grep: no sowohl...als auch, einerseits...andererseits, zwar...aber, genitive relative as key, indirect questions with ob): add one item each, plus reflexive-pronoun case, da-compounds by verb, relative with preposition. Example SINGLE_CHOICE: "Die Kollegin, ___ Projekt wir uebernehmen, ist im Urlaub." (deren / dessen / der / die).
5. **Workplace reading set (4 new stimuli):** business email with a delay problem (5 SINGLE_CHOICE), training programme with eligibility questions, internal notice, instructions (TRUE_FALSE_NG). Add a selective MATCHING with a "none fits" target: 8 situations, 10 short programme texts. Target total reading text about 2,000+ words.
6. **Workplace listening:** (a) 5 short announcements (hotline, delivery notice, schedule change) with TRUE_FALSE; (b) a voice-mail with note fields as GAP_FILL (name, reason, number) [GAP_FILL on a LISTENING stimulus: not verified that it renders]; (c) a four-person meeting recorded with two TTS voices, named roles, one SINGLE_CHOICE per speaker stance.
7. **Stimulus-based writing:** put the advert or incoming email inside the prompt, use telc's "3 of 4 points or 2 plus one own aspect" rubric; add one information-request letter, two complaint letters (service, delivery), one reply to a client email with margin notes (60-80 words), one internal note. Original example: "Sie haben bei einer Firma einen Bildschirm bestellt, geliefert wurde ein anderes Modell. Schreiben Sie an den Kundenservice: Beschreiben Sie das Problem, nennen Sie Ihre Erwartung, fordern Sie eine Loesung, sagen Sie, was Sie sonst tun."
8. **Speaking tasks that simulate interaction:** (a) discussion on a short article (about 120 words) printed in the prompt; (b) joint planning: "Ein Kollege will die Weihnachtsfeier im Betrieb organisieren; er schlaegt X vor, aber Y spricht dagegen. Machen Sie zwei Vorschlaege und reagieren Sie auf den Einwand."; (c) supplier phone call (opposed interests); (d) describe a small table or chart (ZDfB).
9. **Fix the defects in section 3** (items 4-7): remove the duplicate je/desto, replace the nonsense decoy at 196, rerate 171-173 and 218 as lower level, retag misfiled difficulty, and reword metalinguistic items as in-context gaps.
10. **Add item-level quality rules for the seed validator** (not run): no distractor that is a non-word, at most one agreement-only distractor per gap, no repeated construction within a text, key position balanced over a-d. These come from the telc design (same-family decoys) and from the failures above.

Not verified: C-test gap counts; whether GAP_FILL renders on listening stimuli; how many plays our listening allows (telc plays once); image/chart support in speaking prompts; dictionary status of "zur Verfuegung geben". OCR of the Mit Erfolg models is partly garbled, so the tested-point list from them is from legible gaps only.
