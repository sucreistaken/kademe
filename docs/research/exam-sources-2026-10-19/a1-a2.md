## 1. Source inventory

**OSD Zertifikat A1 (ZA1)** [read directly]
- Lesen, 25 min, 3 tasks x 10 pts. T1: match 5 situations to 6 ads (one spare). T2: 3 short ads, 2 yes/no questions each (6 items). T3: match 5 signs/notices to 6 pictures (one spare). Texts are 15-45 words each. Scoring: 5 correct = 10, 4 = 8 ... 0 = 0 (2 pts per item). Pass: 6 of 30 per skill (as printed).
- Horen, ca. 10 min, 3 tasks x 10 pts. T1: five mini-texts matched to six photos, heard once, 30 s preview. T2: voice message, notes sheet (what, weekday, date, time, street, phone number), heard twice; spelled data and the number must be fully right for the 2 pts; grammar and spelling errors are ignored. T3: five street-interview speakers, one box each from four options, heard once. Pass: 6 of 30.
- Schreiben, 20 min, 15 pts. T1 (5 pts): fill in a registration form with own or invented data. T2 (10 pts): answer a friend's email with 3 questions, about 30 words, plus a closing greeting.
- Sprechen, ca. 10 min, 25 pts. T1: introduce yourself, pick 4 of 7 topic words, partner may ask. T2: describe one of three pictures (what, how many people, where, doing what). T3: act out the situation of the picture you chose.

**OSD Zertifikat A2 (ZA2)** [read directly]
- Lesen, 30 min, 25 pts. T1 (15 pts): match 5 short texts (about 40-90 words, labelled with the source country) to 10 headlines (2 topic pairs act as traps, e.g. "bicycle: health" against "bicycle: repair"). Letters may repeat. T2 (10 pts): one 190-word human-interest article, 5 three-option MC items with a worked example. Pass: 5.
- Horen, ca. 15 min, 30 pts. T1: two texts with the same content (e.g. a weather report in two versions), tick exactly 4 of 8 icons; one extra tick costs 3 pts, more than 5 ticks = 0. T2: voice message, 5-line notes sheet (day, food, euros, call-after time, phone number), heard twice; times and the number must be exact. T3: five interview speakers, multiple answers per person from 5 sports, heard once; 2 pts per row, 1 pt if one wrong tick. Pass: 6 of 30.
- Schreiben, 30 min, 15 pts: reply to a friend's email, about 50 words, answer 5 margin questions, add a greeting.
- Sprechen, ca. 10 min, 20 pts: T1 introduce yourself, pick 5 of 6 topics; T2 plan a class trip with a card of options (where, when, how, what to take), 10 min preparation. No picture task.

**DTZ transcripts (A2-B1 prep, 4 model tests)** [read listening transcripts only]
- Listening has 20 numbered items: items 1-9 are short independent texts (answerphone, station and tram announcements, traffic and weather reports, radio programme, shop promotion); 10-17 are four service or private dialogues with 2 items each; 18-20 are a longer discussion or statements. Track lengths run about 1:40 for a 2-item dialogue and 5:30-6:20 for blocks; whether those include the repeat is unclear [unverified].
- A recorded oral exam (13 min) and examiner-question tracks exist. I did not analyse the oral exam beyond the track list.

## 2. Item-writing patterns worth adopting

**Distractors**
- At A1 the distractor is a real detail from the same text (a wrong day, the old time that was corrected, the old room). A1 Lesen T1 also uses a plausible but unused ad. The spare ad or picture is part of the task design.
- At A2 headlines come in topical pairs, so a keyword match fails and the learner must read for the main point. Two headlines can share the same noun (bicycle) but differ in text type (repair tip, health claim, survey result).
- Listening items rely on change-of-plan structure: a first plan is rejected and a second is given, with a number or time repeated. DTZ dialogues start from a small misunderstanding that gets resolved (wrong document, wrong date).
- A2 Horen T1 plays the same facts in two registers (e.g. announcer and conversation) so the candidate must integrate, not just spot.

**Difficulty control**
- A1: one fact per sentence, text-order items, numerals and times in digits, copy-able nouns (same word in question and text).
- A2: paraphrase (Supermarkt -> Geschaft, jung -> viel jünger), counter-fact items, "not stated" items, and attitude questions in interviews.
- Scoring rewards the partial case: 1 pt for one extra wrong tick, 0 for a row with two.

**Text types and situations**
- A1 reading: small ads, shop and service notices, signs with a prohibition or request, event flyers, a ticket/offer box, a housing ad, short emails. A1 listening: a car-viewing appointment note, a street survey, short public texts.
- A2: regional newspaper blurbs, a human-interest story, weather, a farewell party, a sports survey. DTZ adds offices, VHS, doctor, hotel booking, traffic.
- Register: reading texts are authentic-looking, never "textbook". Contact data (email, phone, opening hours) sits in the text and is what is asked.

**Listening scripts**
- Each is short and one-purpose: 30-60 s for announcements, about 1-1.5 min for dialogues. Normal DTZ turns are short (5-15 words) with backchannel words (aha, ach so, gut, also) and corrections. Phone numbers and times are given digit by digit and sometimes repeated.
- Plays: once for most tasks; twice for notes tasks. Preview time of 10-30 s is given before each task.
- Never one long monologue read from a page; the interview-style task is 5 short turns, one speaker per item.

**Writing and speaking**
- Writing prompts show an incoming email with margin questions; the candidate must answer all of them and close with a greeting. A1 is about 30 words with 3 questions; A2 about 50 words with 5 questions. Form-filling alone is 5 pts (a third of A1 writing).
- Speaking: topic-word cards (A1: choose 4 of 7; A2: choose 5 of 6), picture description with fixed question bullets (A1 only), and, at A2, a planning dialogue off a card of options. All speaking is interactive; the partner may ask follow-ups.

**Concrete weaknesses (each read at the line)**
1. TRUE_FALSE_NG offers a "nicht im Text" option but never tests it at A1: prompt at a1.ts:330 and a1.ts:550, and no NG key exists anywhere in a1.ts (grep for "NG" returns nothing). At A2 NG appears exactly once (a2.ts:484, reading); the listening TFNG prompt at a2.ts:647 offers NG but no listening key uses it. Candidates learn that NG is never right, and A1 learners are being asked a three-way decision the A1 exams do not use (both A1 exams use two-way yes/no or right/wrong).
2. The three ad-selection items always have the fourth option "Keine Anzeige" (a1.ts:375-378) and its key is never d: 3 of 3 items. It is a free eliminated option and the item is really 3-option.
3. Giveaway distractors: "acht halb" (a1.ts:185) is non-German and visibly wrong; "links" as a train-class position (a1.ts:674) can't be a location in a "front / back" announcement; "einen Kuli" (a1.ts:476; also the speaking prompt at a1.ts:962) is regional and the other options are semantically stranger than the key. [low severity]
4. A1 reading texts are 54-72 words (stimuli at a1.ts:396-460) against the 15-45 word items in ZA1; the SMS and email need more reading than the exam's A1 level. The bank has no sign/notice, no one-line ad and no price or timetable text, which together are about 2/3 of ZA1 Lesen.
5. Listening scripts are written as prose. The A1 station announcement is 6 separate "Ansage:" lines (a1.ts:601): TTS will insert turn breaks in a single-speaker text [unverified]. None has a repeat, hesitation, correction or backchannel word; the A2 mailbox is the one good exception (it repeats the phone number, a2.ts:653).
6. A2 listening is three long scripts (139, 148, 246 words) with 3-4 items each, against five or more short independent texts in DTZ and ZA2. The interview (a2.ts:655) is 246 words; ZA2's interview is five short speaker turns. Items from the same script share one comprehension point of failure.
7. No listening item tests note-taking (SHORT_TEXT / GAP_FILL for a number, time, name), although this is 10 of 30 pts in both ZA1 and ZA2 Horen. Whether GAP_FILL or SHORT_TEXT is allowed for LISTENING in our importer is [unverified].
8. No headline-matching, no situation-to-ad matching with a spare option at A1 (the ZA1 T1 format), and no multi-select listening (ZA2 T1 and T3). MATCHING (a2.ts:567) has only 3 left items against a 6-ad set.
9. A2 writing never asks the candidate to answer an incoming message with questions; all six prompts (a2.ts:836-950) give bullet instructions only. The exam format requires answering all questions from a quoted email. A1 writing has 2 forms (a1.ts:750+) built as "fill in the form for a colleague", which is a different skill from filling in your own data.
10. A2 speaking has one planning item (a2.ts:982+) and it is a one-sided voicemail. ZA2 T2 is a two-way planning dialogue from an options card. No A1 picture/situation prompt (ZA1 T2/T3); only possible via text since we have no images.
11. Policy tension: the A2 interview centres on age (title and the "viel jünger" statement at a2.ts:780), while the A1 header says age is left out on purpose because the bank is also used in hiring. Worth a decision, not clearly a bug.
12. Low-risk key tension: a2.ts:556 (Kurs 4). The programme says all courses are in working hours, yet Kurs 3 is 16:00-17:30; the answer still follows from the stated hours of the person. I would not change the key, only drop the generic line from the text.

## 4. Top 10 recommendations (ranked, existing item types only)

1. **Split long texts into many short stimuli; keep at most 2 items per text.** Aim for 8-10 reading and 6-8 listening stimuli per level. Target 15-45 words at A1, 40-110 at A2 (TFNG, SC, MATCHING).
2. **Make TFNG honest.** A1: only R/F (change the prompt, drop the NG wording). A2: use NG in about 1 of 4 statements, in reading and listening. Example NG: text says the course starts in October; statement "Die Gebühr ist 40 Euro" is not mentioned.
3. **Add sign/notice/one-line-ad reading items at A1** (SC with 3 options, no sign-image needed): "Hunde bitte an die Leine." -> "Was ist hier erlaubt?" a) Hunde ohne Leine b) Hunde mit Leine c) keine Hunde.
4. **Add situation-to-ad MATCHING at A1** (5 situations, 6 ads, one spare) and **headline-to-text MATCHING at A2** (5 texts, 8-10 headlines, topic pairs as traps; letters may repeat).
5. **Note-taking listening via SHORT_TEXT or GAP_FILL** (name, weekday, time, phone number; accept digit or word forms). Confirm the importer allows it for LISTENING first [unverified]. Example gap: "Die Praxis ist am ___ zu." with a script that says Mittwoch after a correction.
6. **Rewrite listening scripts as speech.** Short turns (5-15 words), a correction ("nicht um acht, sondern um halb neun"), one repeated number, and 1-2 backchannel words (Aha, Ach so). Keep a single speaker label per announcement as one line, not six.
7. **Add MULTI_CHOICE listening** the ZA2 way: "Welche vier Aussagen stimmen?" over 8 options, or a per-speaker multi-answer grid as 5 short SC/MC items (2 pts for exact, 1 for partial if the scorer supports it [unverified]).
8. **Remove free-elimination distractors.** Replace "Keine Anzeige" with a real fourth ad, replace "acht halb" with "halb acht" style plausible traps (e.g. "Viertel acht"), and drop "links".
9. **Writing: quote the incoming message in the prompt with 3 (A1) or 5 (A2) margin questions**, require all to be answered plus a greeting, 30 / 50 words. Example A2 stem: Freund schreibt: "Ich ziehe um. Hast du am Samstag Zeit? Kannst du ein Auto leihen? Was machst du sonst am Wochenende?" The rubric already has contentPoints; add a word-count floor (about 50% of target gives 0 on Erfüllung, as in the Goethe spec).
10. **Speaking: add a topic-card prompt (pick any 4 of 7 words, A1) and a two-turn planning prompt with an options card (where / when / how / what to take, A2)** modelled as SPEAKING_PROMPT with a recorded partner line in the stem. Decide separately whether age and origin stay out of hiring-facing content (finding 11).
