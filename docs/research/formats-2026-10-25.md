# Formats of the starter bank against the model tests (2026-10-25)

Owner (round 3, 2026-10-25): "We had our books; don't hesitate to take different formats from them; it has to be a
comprehensive exam. The questions and patterns could be written more properly." This applies to the placement test
and to every level-verification form.

Method. The format list of each exam comes from `docs/research/exam-sources-2026-10-19/` (read from the ÖSD
Modellsätze A1-C2, telc B1/B2/Beruf, TestDaF and prep books in `~/Desktop/Projects/kademe-kaynaklar/text/`), with
spot checks in the Modellsatz texts for the formats this round took (marked "checked"). Sources are copyrighted:
only formats and item-writing patterns were used, nothing was copied, `pnpm bank:originality` reports 0 matches.
The bank side comes from `pnpm exam:format-inventory` (offline: the bank by task pattern, and 300 first-time forms per
template and level drawn with the engine's own draw, as `exam:form-timing` does). "Format" is the engine's item format
(what form drawing mixes); "pattern" is finer, the task as a candidate would name it, worked out in the script from the
item type, the content and the prompt. Before = `platform/solutions` at `72fdb20`, after = this round.

## 2. The bank before this round: what was thin or missing

Round 1 (2026-10-23) already shipped situation-to-advert matching with "none fits", headline matching at A2, insertion
(B2-C2), opinion matching, Ja/Nein, tick-n, picture tasks, table completion, form filling, Sprachbausteine 15, open
cloze, chart writing. What the inventory still shows:

- **Reading.** Headline matching was one item at B1 and B2 and none at C1 (ÖSD ZB2/ZC1 and telc use it at every level
  from A2). Nothing asked "is this person for or against?" (ÖSD B1 Lesen 4, the only source format with Ja/Nein per
  person). Reading held 11 to 13 texts per level from B1; C2 reading held exactly 24 items in 12 texts, so three forms of eight
  items left no slack (`EXPECTED_SHORTFALL`).
- **Listening.** No "who says what" below B2 (ÖSD B1 Hören 4, ZA2 Hören 3). Few single-purpose short announcements with
  one or two questions (telc B1/B2 and DTZ build whole papers from them): none at B1. No "correct the printed details"
  (ÖSD C2 Hören 1, ZDfB). Note taking was one typed gap item at B2. B1 had 8 clips only. A1 listening was one clip
  short of three forms.
- **Language.** Every cloze and word-bank text was an article or a story; telc Sprachbausteine are letters and
  "advert plus reply". Level-check grammar holds single items only, and A1 and C2 had 24 each, three short of three
  forms (`EXPECTED_SHORTFALL` listed them).
- **Writing.** Replies to an incoming message with questions or margin notes: one item at A1, A2, B1 and C2, none at
  B2 and C1 (ÖSD B2 Schreiben 1, C1 Schreiben 1). One B2 complaint, none at C1. No promotional text from a fact sheet
  (ÖSD C2), no C2 data task.
- **Speaking.** No phone call to negotiate or apologise at B2 and C1 (ÖSD C1 Sprechen 1, telc B2 Beruf).
- **Forms.** A first-time placement form saw letter-style cloze in 34% of forms (A1 only), who-says-what in listening in
  55%, no yes/no for/against comments at all.

## 6. Instruction wording (Aufgabenstellungen)

House style in `src/db/seed-bank/instructions.ts` (table of every task type), pinned by `instructions.test.ts`:
formal Sie, verb first ("Lesen Sie ...", "Hören Sie ..."), the question, then the rule of the task ("Jede Anzeige passt
nur einmal. Zwei Sätze passen nicht."); the answer words of the screen (Richtig / Falsch / Nicht im Text, Ja / Nein /
Text sagt dazu nichts) instead of (R), (F), (NG); no "ziehen"; every stated count (spare headlines, missing
sentences) is checked against the key. Done:

- True/false and Ja/Nein sets: 60 prompts to the fixed lines (reading says "Lesen Sie den Text", listening "Hören Sie
  den Text"; 33 reading and 21 listening true/false, 6 reading Ja/Nein).
- Matching: 50 prompts (40 reading, 10 listening: situation to advert, headline to text, sentence and paragraph
  insertion, who says what, section assignment, course to person) with a lead sentence and true counts; seven listening
  prompts (four C1 "Wer vertritt welche Ansicht?", three C2) had no real instruction.
- Note taking: 13 prompts ("Hören Sie die Nachricht. Ergänzen Sie die Notizen.").
- Tick-n: 3 prompts lose the sentence "Kreuzen Sie vier Antworten an" because the screen already prints it.
- Total 126 existing prompts changed (READING 79, LISTENING 47). A prompt is part of the seed key, so
  `bank:topup --sync-seed` cannot update it in place: each becomes a new item (answer keys unchanged), and
  `--retire-stale` retires the old one. Trial on a copy of `kademe_platform`: insert 220 items (126 reworded + 94 new)
  and 33 texts and clips, retire 126, approved starter items 660 -> 754, a second run changes nothing.
- Left as they were because they already read like the exams: single-choice questions, cloze, word bank, open cloze and
  C-test instructions, writing and speaking prompts.
