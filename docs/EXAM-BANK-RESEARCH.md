# German (DaF) Item Bank: Research Spec, A1 to C2

Status: research note, 2026-10-07. Purpose: a sourced specification for writing a German placement and level-verification bank with five sections (GRAMMAR, READING, LISTENING, WRITING, SPEAKING).

**How to read the confidence markers**

- **[V]** verified: I read it directly in the official document text (Goethe Modellsätze A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2 and the BAMF/Goethe DTZ handbook, downloaded and text-extracted on 2026-10-07).
- **[S]** secondary: taken from search summaries of official pages or from prep sites. The figures are probably right, but I did not read the primary PDF.
- **[D]** derived: my own synthesis from standard DaF curricula and the Profile deutsch categories. Profile deutsch itself is a paid book plus CD-ROM, and I did not have access to its database. Treat these as an informed proposal, not as a quotation.

All example items below are my own wording, written for this spec. None are copied from test material.

---

## 0. Cross-level facts that shape the whole bank

- **Scoring of the receptive skills.** Goethe certificates score each module out of 100 and pass at 60 (B1 Modellsatz: "maximal 100 Punkte pro Modul ... Bestehensgrenze 60 Punkte") [V]. telc B1 has 300 points: 225 written and 75 oral, and each part needs 60 % with no compensation between them [S].
- **Rubric family.** The Goethe Schreiben and Sprechen criteria are the same family at every level from A2 to C1 [V]:
  - **Erfüllung / Aufgabenerfüllung**: content, length, the required speech acts (Sprachfunktionen), text type and register.
  - **Kohärenz**: text structure and linking.
  - **Wortschatz**: range (Spektrum) and control (Beherrschung).
  - **Strukturen**: range and control of morphology, syntax and orthography.
  - **Speaking adds** Interaktion (start, keep going, close, react), Aussprache (Satzmelodie, Wortakzent, Einzellaute) and, at C1, Flüssigkeit.
  - **Bands and the gate.** Each criterion is graded A, B, C, D or E. If Erfüllung gets E (for example less than 50 % of the word count, or off topic), the whole task scores 0.
  - **What the bands measure.** The descriptors move from "differenziert" to "überwiegend angemessen" to "teilweise angemessen oder begrenzt" to "kaum vorhanden". Errors are judged by their effect on comprehension ("vereinzelte Fehlgriffe beeinträchtigen das Verständnis nicht"), not by counting them.
- **Vocabulary ceilings.**
  - Goethe A1 word list: about 650 words. Learners should understand all of them passively and have about half available actively [V].
  - Goethe B1 word list: about 2,400 lexical units [V].
  - The A2 figure I have seen quoted (about 1,300) is **not verified**.
  - Rule borrowed from the DTZ handbook: the parts of a text that are relevant to the solution use only listed vocabulary, while the parts that are not relevant may be authentic and unrestricted [V].
- **Handlungsfelder (BAMF Rahmencurriculum / DTZ), as listed in the DTZ handbook** [V]: Ämter und Behörden, Arbeit, Arbeitssuche, Aus- und Weiterbildung, Banken und Versicherungen, Betreuung und Ausbildung der Kinder, Einkaufen, Gesundheit, Mediennutzung, Mobilität, Unterricht, Wohnen. Cross-field communication covers social contact, feelings and opinions, conflict and dissent, dealing with the migration situation, and language learning. **For a hiring context, weight these fields highest:** Arbeit, Arbeitssuche, Aus- und Weiterbildung, Mediennutzung and Ämter.

## 1. Per-level specification

### A1 (Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1)

**Exam format** [V], from Übungssatz 02:

| Module | Time | Parts and tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Hören | ~20 min, 15 items | Teil 1: 6 short everyday dialogues, a/b/c, played twice. Teil 2: 4 public announcements (station, airport, shop), richtig/falsch, played once. Teil 3: 5 phone or answerphone messages, a/b/c, played twice. |
| Lesen | ~25 min, 15 items | Teil 1: 2 short emails or notes, R/F. Teil 2: choose the right ad or web page from a or b ("Wo finden Sie Informationen?"). Teil 3: signs and notices, R/F. |
| Schreiben | ~20 min | Teil 1: fill 5 gaps in a form from a short narrative. Teil 2: short message of about 30 words covering 3 guiding points (for example: why you are writing, you will come later, ask whether you can help). |
| Sprechen | ~15 min, in a group | Teil 1: introduce yourself (name, age, country, home, languages, job, hobbies). Teil 2: ask for and give information using topic and word cards. Teil 3: make and respond to requests using picture cards. |

**Grammar inventory** [D, consistent with the DTZ structures list]

- **Present tense, regular and irregular** (e.g. fahren > fährt, essen > isst; sein, haben).
  - `Er ___ jeden Tag mit dem Bus. (a) fahrt (b) fährt (c) fahren`
- **Verb second, verb first in yes/no questions, W-questions.**
  - `___ wohnst du? - In Köln. (a) Wo (b) Wer (c) Wie`
- **Nominative and accusative, with definite, indefinite and negative articles (kein).**
  - `Ich habe ___ Bruder. (a) ein (b) einen (c) einem`
- **Modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen, möchten) and the verb bracket.**
  - `Ich ___ heute nicht kommen. (a) kann (b) kannst (c) können`
- **Separable verbs (anrufen, aufstehen, einkaufen).**
  - `Wann stehst du morgen ___? (a) an (b) auf (c) aus`
- **Possessive articles in the nominative and accusative.**
  - `Das ist Anna und das ist ___ Mann. (a) ihr (b) sein (c) ihre`
- **Imperative in the Sie and du forms.**
  - `___ Sie bitte langsam! (a) Sprechen (b) Sprecht (c) Sprich`
- **Perfekt with haben and sein, frequent verbs only (receptive, plus a few active forms).**
  - `Gestern ___ ich nach Berlin gefahren. (a) habe (b) bin (c) war`
- **Prepositions of time (am, um, im) and of place (in, nach, aus) as fixed chunks; nicht versus kein; plural of frequent nouns; numbers, time and date.**

**Topics** [S/V]: personal details, family, home, food and shopping, daily routine, leisure, travel and transport, health basics, work and job titles. Wortgruppen [V] include numbers, weekdays, months, colours, times and countries.

**Reading in the bank**
- Text types: notes, SMS, short emails, signs, ads and timetables.
- Length: 20 to 80 words per text.
- Items: R/F, choose between 2 texts, 3-option MC.
- Write 1 item per sentence of information, and give every item a distinct location in the text.

**Listening in the bank**
- Text types: dialogues of 3 to 6 turns, announcements, answerphone messages.
- Length: 15 to 45 seconds. Speed: slow and clearly articulated.
- Plays: twice for dialogues and messages, once for announcements, mirroring Goethe.
- Use 2 voices (male and female) for dialogues and 1 voice for announcements and messages.

**Writing in the bank**
- Task 1: a form with 5 slots.
- Task 2: about 30 words, 3 guiding points, informal or semi-formal.
- Rubric: the Goethe A1 Schreiben sheet scores Teil 2 by fulfilment of the guiding points plus communicative design [S]. In the bank, score it as **Aufgabenerfüllung (3 points) + Kommunikative Gestaltung / Sprache (2 points)**. That split is my reconstruction; the score sheet only shows "3 / 1,5 / 0" per point [V].

**Speaking in the bank**
- About 60 seconds to introduce yourself on camera.
- 2 or 3 single-response prompts: ask a question about a key word ("Wochenende?"), and make a request based on a picture.
- Score: task fulfilment, intelligibility, basic structures.

### A2 (Goethe-Zertifikat A2)

**Exam format** [V], from Modellsatz Erwachsene:

| Module | Time | Parts and tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 30 min, 20 items | Teil 1: media text, 3-option MC, 5 items. Teil 2: information boards and event programmes, MC, 5 items. Teil 3: correspondence, MC, 5 items. Teil 4: ads, matching, 5 items. |
| Hören | 30 min, 20 items | Teil 1: radio, answerphone and announcements, MC, 5 items, played twice. Teil 2: one connected conversation, picture-text matching, 5 items, played once. Teil 3: 5 short conversations, MC with pictures, played once. Teil 4: radio interview, R/F, 5 items, played twice. |
| Schreiben | 30 min | Teil 1: personal message (Kontaktpflege), 20 to 30 words. Teil 2: semi-formal message (Handlungsregulierung), 30 to 40 words. Each task has 3 speech-act points. Erfüllung gets E if the text is under 50 % of the word count (10 or 15 words). |
| Sprechen | 15 min per pair | Teil 1: exchange personal information using cards. Teil 2: talk about your own life in detail (monologue plus questions from the examiner). Teil 3: plan something together. |

**Grammar inventory** [D, cross-checked against the DTZ list]

- **Perfekt of all frequent verbs; Präteritum of sein, haben and the modal verbs.**
  - `Letztes Jahr ___ wir keine Zeit für Urlaub. (a) hatten (b) haben gehabt (c) hätten`
  - `Als Kind ___ ich nicht schwimmen. (a) konnte (b) kann (c) könnte`
- **Dative (articles, personal pronouns) and verbs with the dative (helfen, gefallen, gehören).**
  - `Kannst du ___ bitte helfen? (a) mich (b) mir (c) meiner`
- **Two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen) with wo? or wohin?, plus stellen/stehen, legen/liegen, hängen.**
  - `Ich lege das Buch auf ___ Tisch. (a) dem (b) den (c) der`
- **Subordinate clauses with weil, dass, wenn and ob, with the verb in final position.**
  - `Ich komme nicht, weil ich krank ___. (a) bin (b) sein (c) bin ich`
- **deshalb and trotzdem with inversion.**
  - `Es regnet, deshalb ___ zu Hause. (a) ich bleibe (b) bleibe ich (c) bleiben ich`
- **Comparative and superlative (gern, lieber, am liebsten; gut, besser, am besten).**
  - `Tee trinke ich ___ als Kaffee. (a) gerner (b) lieber (c) mehr gern`
- **Reflexive verbs (sich freuen, sich treffen, sich interessieren für).**
  - `Wir treffen ___ um acht. (a) uns (b) sich (c) unser`
- **Adjective endings after definite and indefinite articles in the nominative and accusative (receptive); Konjunktiv II for polite requests (hätte gern, würde gern, könnten Sie); verb plus preposition (warten auf, sich freuen auf).**

**Topics**: work and the workplace, housing and moving, health and the doctor, education, shopping and services, travel, media, celebrations.

**Reading in the bank**
- Text types: personal and semi-formal emails, short newspaper or blog items of 100 to 200 words, programme listings, ads.
- Items: MC with 3 options, matching situation to ad with distractor ads included.

**Listening in the bank**
- Text types: radio snippets, answerphone messages, short conversations, a 2 to 3 minute interview.
- Speed: natural but careful.
- Plays: twice for short informational texts, once for conversations.

**Writing in the bank**
- 2 prompts per level slot: one informal of 20 to 30 words, one semi-formal of 30 to 40 words, each with 3 bullet points.
- Rubric [V]: Aufgabenerfüllung (speech acts, register) plus Sprache (range and control of Kohärenz, Wortschatz and Strukturen).

**Speaking in the bank**
- Talk about yourself for 90 seconds (for example: "Erzählen Sie über Ihre Arbeit / Ihren Alltag").
- 1 role-play prompt in which the candidate proposes a time and a place.

### B1 (Goethe-Zertifikat B1)

**Exam format** [V], from Modellsatz Erwachsene:

| Module | Time | Parts and tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min, 30 items | Teil 1: correspondence or blog, 6 R/F items, 10 min. Teil 2: information and argument texts, 3-option MC, 6 items. Teil 3: reading for orientation, match 7 situations to ads (each ad used once, 1 or 2 situations have no matching ad, "0"), 7 items. Teil 4: opinion texts, Ja/Nein, 7 items. Teil 5: written instructions or house rules, MC, 4 items. Teil 1 text: about 320 to 340 words [S]. |
| Hören | 40 min, 30 items | Teil 1: announcements and instructions, R/F plus MC, 10 items, played twice. Teil 2: a guide or talk in front of an audience, MC, 5 items, played once. Teil 3: conversation between native speakers, R/F, 7 items, played once. Teil 4: radio discussion, matching who says what, 8 items, played twice. |
| Schreiben | 60 min | Aufgabe 1: personal email of about 80 words with 3 points (describe, give reasons, propose), 20 min. Aufgabe 2: opinion on a forum or guestbook of about 80 words, 25 min. Aufgabe 3: semi-formal email of about 40 words (apologise, ask for something), 15 min. |
| Sprechen | 15 min per pair, 15 min preparation | Teil 1: plan something together using 4 guiding points, about 3 min. Teil 2: present a topic over 5 given slides, about 3 min. Teil 3: give feedback and ask and answer questions, about 2 min. |

**Rubric** [V]
- **Schreiben**: Erfüllung (content and length, speech acts, Textsorte, register), Kohärenz (Textaufbau, Verknüpfung), Wortschatz (Spektrum, Beherrschung), Strukturen (Spektrum, Beherrschung including orthography).
- **Sprechen**: Erfüllung, Interaktion, Wortschatz, Strukturen and Aussprache. For the presentation, the Kohärenz band depends on how many slides are covered properly ("Alle 5 Folien" gives A).

**Grammar inventory** [D, cross-checked against DTZ items marked B1]

- **Präteritum of frequent verbs in narration; Plusquamperfekt with nachdem and als.**
  - `Nachdem er gegessen ___, ging er spazieren. (a) hat (b) hatte (c) war`
- **Passive (present, Präteritum, with a modal verb).**
  - `Das Paket ___ morgen geliefert. (a) wird (b) ist (c) hat`
  - `Die Wohnung muss noch ___ werden. (a) geputzt (b) putzen (c) geputzen`
- **Relative clauses, including with a preposition, wo and was.**
  - `Das ist der Kollege, ___ ich dir erzählt habe. (a) von dem (b) dem (c) von den`
- **Infinitive clauses: zu, um ... zu, ohne ... zu, statt ... zu.**
  - `Ich lerne Deutsch, ___ in Deutschland zu arbeiten. (a) damit (b) um (c) für`
- **Konjunktiv II for wishes, advice and hypotheticals (wäre, hätte, würde, sollte, könnte).**
  - `Wenn ich mehr Zeit ___, würde ich einen Kurs machen. (a) habe (b) hätte (c) hatte`
- **Two-part connectors: sowohl ... als auch, weder ... noch, entweder ... oder, nicht nur ... sondern auch.**
- **Temporal and concessive connectors: als / wenn, während, bevor, bis, seit, obwohl.**
  - `___ ich jung war, wohnte ich in Graz. (a) Wenn (b) Als (c) Wann`
- **Adjective declension in all cases; Genitiv (receptive, plus wegen and trotz); n-declension.**
- **Prepositional adverbs (darauf, worauf); Futur I for promises and predictions; word formation (-ung, -heit, -bar, -los, un-).**

**Topics**: work and careers, job applications, education and further training, health, the environment, media and technology, living together, travel complaints, culture and leisure.

**Reading in the bank**
- Text types: blog or email of 250 to 350 words, press texts of 300 to 450 words, ad sets of 8 to 10 with distractors, house rules and terms of use.
- Items: R/F, Ja/Nein on opinions, MC with 3 options, matching with "no match" allowed.

**Listening in the bank**
- Text types: announcements of 20 to 40 seconds, a guided tour or talk of 2 to 3 minutes, a conversation of 3 to 4 minutes, a radio discussion of 4 to 5 minutes.
- Speakers: 2 for conversations. For a discussion, simulate a moderator plus 2 guests if the TTS allows a third voice; otherwise build it as an interview.

**Writing in the bank**
- About 80 words informal, about 80 words opinion, about 40 words semi-formal.

**Speaking in the bank**
- Present a topic for 2 to 3 minutes (prompt modelled on the 5 slides: personal experience, situation in your country, pros and cons, your opinion, closing).
- Plus 1 planning prompt in which the candidate makes suggestions and responds to a prerecorded partner turn.

### B2 (Goethe-Zertifikat B2)

**Exam format** [V], from Modellsatz Erwachsene:

| Module | Time | Parts and tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min, 30 items | Teil 1: attitudes, match 9 statements to 4 texts, 18 min. Teil 2: information, gap text with 6 sentences to insert, 12 min. Teil 3: information, 3-option MC, 6 items, 12 min. Teil 4: viewpoints, match 6 items to 8 texts, 12 min. Teil 5: rules and instructions, match 3 paragraphs, 6 min. |
| Hören | 40 min, 30 items | Teil 1: 5 everyday conversations or statements, R/F plus MC, 10 items, played once. Teil 2: radio interview with an expert, MC, 6 items, played twice. Teil 3: radio talk with several people, "Wer sagt das?", 6 items, played once. Teil 4: short lecture, MC, 8 items, played twice. |
| Schreiben | 75 min | Teil 1: forum post giving and justifying an opinion and discussing pros and cons, about 150 words, 50 min. Teil 2: personal or semi-formal message (explain, describe, propose, request politely), about 100 words, 25 min. |
| Sprechen | 15 min preparation | Teil 1: prepared talk on a chosen topic plus follow-up questions, about 4 min per candidate (8 min for both). Teil 2: discussion of a controversial question in which each candidate argues a position, about 5 min. |

**Grammar inventory** [D]

- **Passive in all tenses, Zustandspassiv, and passive substitutes (sein + zu + infinitive, sich lassen, -bar).**
  - `Das Problem ___ sich leicht lösen. (a) lässt (b) wird (c) ist`
  - `Die Formulare sind bis Freitag ___. (a) abzugeben (b) abgeben (c) abgegeben zu`
- **Konjunktiv II in the past (hätte ... gemacht, wäre ... gekommen) and with modals (hätte ... müssen).**
  - `Du ___ früher anrufen sollen. (a) hättest (b) wärst (c) würdest`
- **Konjunktiv I for reported speech (receptive and active for er/sie: sei, habe, könne).**
  - `Der Chef sagte, er ___ morgen nicht im Büro. (a) ist (b) sei (c) wäre gewesen`
- **Extended participial attributes (receptive) and Partizip I and II as adjectives.**
  - `die ___ Preise (a) steigenden (b) gestiegen (c) steigend`
- **Nominalisation and verbalisation (wegen + Genitiv versus weil); Funktionsverbgefüge (in Frage kommen, zur Verfügung stellen, Entscheidung treffen).**
  - `Wir müssen bis Montag eine Entscheidung ___. (a) machen (b) treffen (c) geben`
- **Connectors: je ... desto, falls, sofern, indem, sodass, anstatt dass, zwar ... aber, einerseits ... andererseits.**
  - `Je mehr man übt, ___ sicherer wird man. (a) umso (b) als (c) wie`
- **Genitive prepositions (aufgrund, innerhalb, trotz, während, statt); verbs, nouns and adjectives with fixed prepositions; modal particles (receptive).**

**Topics**: the working world (home office, career change), studies, the environment and sustainability, society and consumption, technology and the media, health, housing models, science popularisation.

**Reading in the bank**
- Text types: opinion statements of 80 to 120 words each in sets of 4 to 8, articles of 400 to 600 words, sentence-gap texts.
- Items: MC with 3 options, sentence insertion with 1 or 2 extra sentences, matching with more texts than items.

**Listening in the bank**
- Text types: a 4 to 6 minute interview, a 3 to 4 minute lecture, short everyday statements.
- Speed: normal native tempo.
- 2 voices are sufficient for all B2 tasks.

**Writing in the bank**
- About 150 words for a forum opinion with 4 content points, about 100 words for a semi-formal message.

**Speaking in the bank**
- A 3 to 4 minute talk with structure: introduce, describe, argue, conclude.
- A discussion prompt answered against a recorded counter-argument.
- Rubric [V, B2 sheet names]: Erfüllung, Kohärenz, Wortschatz, Strukturen, Aussprache, plus Interaktion for the discussion.

### C1 (Goethe-Zertifikat C1)

**Exam format** [V], from Modellsatz:

| Module | Time | Parts and tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min, 30 items | Teil 1: popular-science article, gap text with 4-option MC (word level), 8 items, 10 min. Teil 2: magazine article dense with information, 3-option MC, 7 items, 20 min. Teil 3: commentary or report, gap text with 8 sentences to insert, 20 min. Teil 4: (popular) science contributions, match 5 positions or statements, 7 items, 15 min. |
| Hören | ~40 min, 30 items | Teil 1: podcast reviewing books, match statements to sections, 6 items, played once. Teil 2: expert interview, MC, 9 items, played twice. Teil 3: radio discussion with 3 people, MC, 8 items, played once in 4 sections. Teil 4: lecture, MC, 7 items, played twice. |
| Schreiben | 75 min | Teil 1: forum discussion contribution of about 230 words carrying 4 speech acts (explain, argue, illustrate with examples...), 50 min. Teil 2: (semi-)formal email of about 120 words (raise a problem, propose, describe, show understanding politely), 25 min. |
| Sprechen | ~20 min per pair, 20 min preparation | Teil 1: talk of about 5 minutes with bullet points plus questions. Teil 2: free discussion of a controversial question based on a short input text and 4 bullet points, about 5 min. |

**Rubric** [V, C1 Sprechen]: Aufgabenerfüllung; Kohärenz of the talk (Aufbau, Verknüpfung, **Flüssigkeit**); questions and answers on the talk; Interaktion in the discussion; register and sociocultural appropriateness; Wortschatz and Strukturen (range now "breit, differenziert"); Aussprache.

**Grammar inventory** [D]

- **Full nominal style: nominalisation and reverse transformation of verbal and nominal style.**
  - `Nach ___ des Vertrags begann die Arbeit. (a) Unterzeichnung (b) unterzeichnen (c) Unterzeichnen`
- **Extended attribute (active); Partizipialkonstruktionen replacing relative clauses.**
  - `die von der Firma im letzten Jahr ___ Maßnahmen (a) eingeführten (b) eingeführt (c) einführenden`
- **Modal verbs used subjectively (soll, will, dürfte, müsste, kann ... haben) to express assumption or hearsay.**
  - `Er ___ das Geld gestohlen haben, behauptet die Polizei. (a) soll (b) will (c) muss`
- **Konjunktiv I and II in indirect speech, including the substitution rules.**
- **Advanced connectors and prepositions: insofern als, zumal, wohingegen, geschweige denn; angesichts, hinsichtlich, zufolge, mangels, ungeachtet.**
  - `___ der hohen Kosten wurde das Projekt gestoppt. (a) Angesichts (b) Obwohl (c) Trotzdem`
- **Collocations and Funktionsverbgefüge at depth; idiomatic fixed expressions; register shifts (formal versus colloquial).**
  - `Das Thema wurde zur Sprache ___. (a) gebracht (b) gekommen (c) gestellt`
- **Word formation (nominal composition, prefix verbs with nuances: be-, ver-, er-, zer-).**

**Topics**: science and research, economy and the world of work, society and politics, education, the environment, culture and media, health and psychology.

**Reading in the bank**
- Text types: articles of 500 to 900 words, a word-level cloze (4 options), sentence- or paragraph-insertion gaps, multi-text positions.

**Listening in the bank**
- Text types: an interview or podcast of 5 to 8 minutes, a lecture of 5 to 7 minutes, a 3-person discussion (degrade to 2 voices if needed).
- Use authentic tempo, some hedging and discourse markers.

**Writing in the bank**
- About 230 words of argumentative forum text, about 120 words of semi-formal email.

**Speaking in the bank**
- A 4 to 5 minute talk from bullet points.
- A recorded discussion turn reacting to an input text.

### C2 (Goethe-Zertifikat C2: Großes Deutsches Sprachdiplom)

**Exam format** [V], from Modellsatz Vs14:

| Module | Time | Parts and tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 80 min | Teil 1: commentary, 4-option MC, 10 items, 25 min. Teil 2: article, match 7 of 9 statements to sections, 20 min. Teil 3: report with paragraphs removed, insert them (one extra), 25 min. Teil 4: skim 4 job ads and match, 10 min. |
| Hören | ~35 min | Teil 1: 5 radio excerpts, 3 R/F-type items each, played once, ~12 min. Teil 2: conversation, decide whether an opinion belongs to one speaker or is shared by both, played once, ~5 min. Teil 3: interview with an expert, MC, played twice, ~18 min. |
| Schreiben | 80 min | Teil 1: rewrite marked passages of a short paper using given words that must not be changed (a transformation task), 20 min. Teil 2: about 350 words, a reader's letter referring to 3 statements or a book review covering 3 content aspects, 60 min. |
| Sprechen | ~15 min, 15 min preparation | Teil 1: about 5 minutes of production on a topic, weighing positions with quotations as input, then questions. Teil 2: about 5 minutes of interaction with the examiner. |

**Grammar inventory** [D]: everything above, used flexibly, plus:

- **Paraphrase and transformation tasks** (the key C2 grammar format), for example rewriting a sentence while keeping the meaning and using a given word.
  - `Obwohl er sich bemühte, scheiterte er. (trotz) -> Trotz seiner Bemühungen scheiterte er.`
- **Stylistic register control: Konjunktiv I in formal reporting; elaborate nominal style versus deliberate verbal style.**
- **Idioms, proverbs and phraseology; fine lexical distinctions (scheinbar versus anscheinend, umfahren versus umfahren).**
  - `Er hat den Termin ___ verpasst. Er wollte nicht hingehen. (a) absichtlich (b) versehentlich (c) zufällig`
- **Rare structures: Konjunktiv I in commands and wishes (Man nehme...); concessive constructions (so ... auch, wie ... auch immer); inverted conditionals without wenn (Sollte es regnen, ...).**
  - `___ Sie Fragen haben, wenden Sie sich an uns. (a) Sollten (b) Würden (c) Wenn`

**Reading in the bank**: commentaries and essays of 700 to 1,200 words, implicit attitude and irony, 4-option MC, paragraph insertion.

**Listening in the bank**: radio features, debates, interviews at full speed with overlapping viewpoints; tasks on agreement or disagreement and implied stance.

**Writing in the bank**
- A transformation set (5 to 10 sentences).
- An argumentative or review text of about 350 words.

**Speaking in the bank**: a 5-minute monologue weighing 3 quotations, plus a recorded follow-up question.

### Other providers (for cross-checking)

- **telc Deutsch B1 / Zertifikat Deutsch** [S]
  - Leseverstehen: 3 parts (global matching of 5 texts to 10 headings, detail MC with 5 items, selective matching of 10 situations to 12 ads).
  - Sprachbausteine: 2 parts (grammar cloze with 3 options, 10 items; vocabulary cloze from a 15-word bank, 10 items). This is the closest official model for our GRAMMAR section.
  - Hörverstehen: 3 parts, R/F.
  - Writing: 1 letter with 4 content points.
  - Oral: paired.
- **telc C1 Hochschule** [S]: Sprachbausteine with 22 four-option items in 22 min. Reading has 3 parts in 90 min, including R/F/"nicht im Text". Listening includes an information-transfer part.
- **ÖSD**: the ZB1 writing email is about 80 words [S]. I found Modellsatz download pages for ZA1 to ZC2 but did not read their structure.
- **DTZ** (BAMF, Goethe and telc) [V]
  - It is a **scaled A2 to B1 test**: one paper reports either A2 or B1.
  - Hören: 4 tasks, 20 items, each text played once at natural tempo.
  - Items were placed on levels by expert judgement and then confirmed by Rasch analysis against an anchor test, and they are ordered by ascending difficulty. This is the right model for a placement bank (see section 2).

## 2. Placement and level tests versus certificate exams

1. **Purpose and scale.**
   - A certificate (Goethe B1) is a single-level mastery test: almost all items sit around B1 and it reports pass or fail at 60 %.
   - A placement test must **spread item difficulty across the whole A1 to C2 range** and report a level, often down to sub-levels. The Goethe online Einstufungstest reports sub-levels such as A1.2 or B2.1 [S].
   - The DTZ shows the middle way: a scaled test whose items span two levels and are ordered from easy to hard [V].
2. **Discrimination between adjacent levels.**
   - An item earns its place only if it separates level N-1 from level N.
   - Calibrate empirically with Rasch or IRT, as DTZ and Cambridge do. Cambridge rescales logits to mean 100 and SD 20 and links them to CEFR [S].
   - Until real response data exists, tag each item with its intended level plus a "boundary" (for example A2/B1) and treat that as a hypothesis.
   - After at least about 100 responses per item, re-estimate the difficulty and drop items with low or negative point-biserial correlation.
3. **Adaptive logic.**
   - In the Goethe Test PRO, everyone starts at the same level and each next task depends on the previous answer [S].
   - Cambridge CATs use banks of "several hundreds of items" across levels and item types [S].
   - Recommended design: a short routing block of 6 to 8 items at A2/B1, then a staircase or IRT selection, stopping when the standard error is under a threshold or after a maximum number of items.
4. **Short items beat long testlets for routing.** Grammar MC and C-test texts give a lot of information per minute.
   - onSET (g.a.s.t.), a validated German placement test, is purely a **C-test** [S]:
     - 8 short texts, each with 20 gaps and 5 minutes; 40 minutes in total; reports levels A2 to C1.
     - The first and last sentences are intact. From the second sentence on, the second half of every second word is deleted.
   - Reading and listening testlets (4 to 8 items on one text) are better for **verification** after routing.
5. **Unambiguity matters more than in a certificate.** In an adaptive test one bad item misroutes the whole path. Each item must have exactly one defensible key, checked by at least 2 reviewers solving it blind. There is no "best answer" logic.
6. **Productive skills (Writing, Speaking) verify, they do not route.** Give them at the provisional level and one level above. Score with the shared rubric family, and confirm the level only when Erfüllung is at least band B and range is at least "überwiegend angemessen".
7. **C-test specifics for the bank.**
   - Text length: 70 to 100 words, 20 to 25 gaps.
   - Deletion rule: keep floor(n/2) letters and blank the rest. For example `Kin___` for Kinder (6 letters: keep 3, blank 3) and `Ha___` for Hause (5 letters: keep 2, blank 3). I believe this matches the classic Klein-Braley rule of deleting the larger half of odd-length words, but I have not checked it against the original source. Whatever rule you pick, apply it to every item.
   - Skip one-letter words, proper names and numbers.
   - Choose topics neutral to background knowledge, with an A1-A2 text, a B1-B2 text and a C1-C2 text.
   - Scoring: exact form only, or accept orthographic variants listed in the key. Decide once and document it.

## 3. Item-writing rules for DaF MC, gap and C-test items

Based on Haladyna, Downing and Rodriguez (2002, 31 guidelines) [S], the ALTE/Council of Europe Manual (2011) [S] and Goethe/DTZ practice [V], adapted to German.

**Stem and key**
- One clearly correct key. The distractors must be wrong in **standard German in this context**, not just less elegant.
- Test one point per item. Do not combine an article error and a verb error in one option.
- Give enough context to disambiguate. For example, Wechselpräposition items need a verb of motion or position in the stem.
- No negative stems ("Was ist NICHT ...") at A1 to B1. If you use one at a higher level, bold it.
- The gap must be the only solution-relevant unknown. The rest of the sentence should be at or below the target level (DTZ rule: solution-relevant vocabulary comes from the list) [V].

**Distractors**
- Plausible distractors come from **typical learner errors**:
  - wrong case (den / dem);
  - wrong conjugation (fahrt / fährt);
  - wrong auxiliary (habe / bin gefahren);
  - false friends;
  - confused connectors (wenn / als / wann; weil / denn / deshalb with word order);
  - analogical forms (gerner).
- Keep options homogeneous in form and length. Never make the key the longest or only "formal" option.
- Avoid "alle / keine der Antworten". Use 3 options at A1 to B2 (as Goethe does) and 4 at C1 to C2 (as Goethe C1 Teil 1 and C2 Teil 1 do) [V].
- Make sure no distractor is accidentally correct in some region or register. Example: `Ich bin gesessen` is standard in southern Germany, Austria and Switzerland, so never use it as a distractor for `habe gesessen`. The same applies to `der/das Joghurt`, `die/das Cola`, `Jänner` and `Januar`.
- Avoid cueing:
  - article-adjective agreement that gives the answer away (`ein___ Auto` before the gap);
  - grammatical mismatch between the stem and only some options;
  - repeating a word from the stem in the key;
  - for gap texts, giving away the key through a pronoun in the next sentence.

**Matching and R/F**
- Matching sets need more options than items (Goethe B1 Teil 3 uses a "no match" answer; B2 Teil 4 has 8 texts for 6 items) [V].
- R/F statements must paraphrase the text, not copy it word for word. Copying lets candidates find the answer without understanding it.

**Standard German and regional neutrality**
- Write in neutral Standarddeutsch. Goethe, ÖSD and telc are pluricentric, so accept Austrian and Swiss variants in open answers.
- Do not test region-specific lexis (Semmel versus Brötchen) or idioms below C1.

**Fairness**
- Balance gender and names of diverse origin; avoid stereotypes.
- No culture-specific trivia or background knowledge. Do not ask about German holidays, politicians or brands, school-system details, or the price of a train ticket.
- Avoid upsetting topics (illness in detail, death, war, religion) as material for placement.

**Listening for TTS**
- Script the full text, including the instructions.
- Insert natural pauses between turns. At B1+, keep fillers and hedges ("also", "na ja").
- Give speakers distinct voices and names that the items can refer to.
- Make the items follow the order of the information in the audio.
- Prevent the answer being audible as an isolated keyword. Use paraphrase plus distractors that are also mentioned in the audio but negated or revised ("erst Dienstag ... nein, doch Mittwoch").

**Review workflow** (ALTE style)
1. Write.
2. Peer-review against this checklist.
3. A second reviewer solves the item without the key.
4. Pretest.
5. Statistical review.
6. Bank.

Version every item and keep its level tag, skill, Handlungsfeld, grammar point and source.

## 4. Recommended item counts for an adaptive bank

**Reasoning**
- A CAT needs a bank several times larger than the number of items a candidate sees, so that it can target items and control exposure (Sympson-Hetter style) [S].
- Rasch calibration needs about 100 or more responses per item [S].
- If a placement session shows about 20 to 30 GRAMMAR items, about 8 to 12 per C-test text, 2 reading testlets and 2 listening testlets, then each level window needs about 5 times what one candidate sees around that level.

| Level | Grammar MC / gap (single items) | C-test texts (20 to 25 gaps) | Reading testlets (text + items) | Listening testlets (script + items) | Writing prompts | Speaking prompts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 60 | 3 | 8 (3 to 5 items each) | 8 (3 to 5 items each) | 6 (form + message) | 6 |
| A2 | 70 | 4 | 8 | 8 | 6 | 6 |
| B1 | 80 | 4 | 10 (4 to 6 items each) | 10 | 8 | 8 |
| B2 | 80 | 4 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 8 |
| C1 | 60 | 3 | 8 (5 to 8 items each) | 8 | 6 | 6 |
| C2 | 40 | 2 | 6 | 6 | 4 | 4 |
| **Total** | **390** | **20** | **50** | **50** | **38** | **38** |

**Why this shape**
- B1 and B2 are the densest because most placements and hiring decisions fall there, and the A2/B1 and B1/B2 boundaries need the most discrimination.
- C2 is thin because few candidates reach it, and its verification should rest mostly on the productive tasks.
- Minimum viable launch: about 40 % of the table (roughly 150 grammar items, 8 C-test texts, 20 reading and 20 listening testlets) is enough for a staircase test. It is not enough for an IRT CAT with exposure control.
- Within each level, cover every grammar point in the inventory at least 3 times with different item surfaces. Spread items over the Handlungsfelder, with no more than 25 % from any single one.
