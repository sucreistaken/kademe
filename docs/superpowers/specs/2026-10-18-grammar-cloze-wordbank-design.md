# Grammar as MC cloze and word bank: design

Date: 2026-10-18. Branch `platform/solutions`. Status: owner decision in chat (AskUserQuestion, "Test görüşü gibi").
Supersedes the GRAMMAR part of `2026-10-15-onset-placement-design.md` (word-part C-tests).

## 1. Why

A school tester sat the live placement exam (`~/Downloads/Kademe Test Revize.docx`). Word-part C-tests
("Sie si[ ] früh aufges[ ]", word endings dragged from a shared pool) confused German learners: completing word
endings mixes spelling, inflection and guessing the stem. The tester asked for:

- **MC cloze** for grammar: the gap is fully empty; clicking it opens 4 options in a panel on the right (like the
  reading layout); the student picks one.
- **Word bank** for vocabulary: drag-and-drop (or click) of whole words from a pool into gaps.

The owner accepted both. The cost is that options can be guessed; section 4 deals with it.

## 2. Item model

Both formats are `GAP_FILL` items, told apart by skill tag. No database migration (content, key and blueprint
config are jsonb; `item_type` is unchanged).

| Kind | Skill tag | Content | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-test (legacy) | `grammar.ctest` | `{{gN}}` markers, gaps by id | answers (part + whole word), distractors |
| MC cloze | `grammar.cloze` | `{{gN}}` markers, every gap has exactly 4 distinct `choices` | one answer per gap, among its choices |
| Word bank | `grammar.wordbank` | `{{gN}}` markers, gaps without choices | one whole-word answer per gap, at least 3 whole-word `distractors`, none equal to an answer |

`textGapKindOf(item)` returns `CTEST | CLOZE | WORDBANK | null`. Shared engine code (blueprint planning, item weight,
exclusion from automatic item selection, review, report) uses `isTextGap`; `isCTest` stays only for C-test-specific
behaviour (stem drawn with its gap, teacher "accept as correct" for typed gaps).

Started C-test attempts keep rendering and scoring exactly as before: invited students keep their
`blueprint_snapshot`, and old C-test items stay ACTIVE in the bank for custom exams.

## 3. Student-facing shape

- MC cloze: German text on the left (sticky), gaps are empty numbered slots. Clicking a gap (or the first empty gap
  by default) shows "Boşluk n" / "Gap n" with 4 option buttons on the right. Picking fills the gap and moves to the
  next empty gap. Keys 1-4 pick. The student can change any gap.
- Word bank: text with empty slots, a pool of whole words below; drag or click a word into a gap, click a filled gap
  to return the word. Each pool word is used at most once.
- Section order: cloze texts easy to hard, then word banks easy to hard, so the student never switches format text by text.
- Blueprint config: `texts?: { level, kind: "CLOZE" | "WORDBANK" }[]`, read by `textPlanOf()` which also reads legacy
  `cTestLevels` / `cTest`.

Starting compositions (the simulation in section 4 decides the final ones):

| Template | GRAMMAR |
|---|---|
| Placement | 30 min: 6 MC cloze texts A1-C2 (about 12 gaps each) + 3 word banks A2, B1, B2 (8 gaps, 4-5 distractors) |
| Quick screen | 15 min: MC cloze A2, B1, B2 + 1 word bank B1 |

## 4. Measurement

- Scoring per gap is unchanged (fraction of correct gaps per text, `item-scoring.ts`).
- The EAP estimate gets a guessing floor: `p = c + (1 - c) * sigma(theta - b)`, with `c = 1/4` for MC cloze,
  `c = 1/pool size` for word bank, and `c = 0` for every existing item type (so old attempts re-estimate identically).
- Text items stay weighted as testlets (`max(1, gaps / 4)`), now for every text-gap kind.
- Simulation (`form-simulation.ts`, `scripts/simulate-placement.ts`) generates answers with guessing and reports exact
  agreement, within one level, and an over-placement rate. Gate: at A1-C1, exact agreement at least the pre-onSET MC
  form minus 0.02 and within-one at least minus 0.01 (that MC form re-simulated with guessing), and A1 over-placement
  at most 5 points above the C-test reference. If the gate fails: more gaps or texts, then re-run.

## 5. Content

Original German texts written for Kademe (no Goethe, telc or publisher material). Distractors are written by Claude,
never generated by Gemini (`item-generation.ts` excludes both tags). Per level used by a template: 3 MC cloze and
3 word-bank texts. A blind-solve pass (an agent without the key) checks that every cloze gap has exactly one fitting
option and no word-bank word fits two gaps. A native German teacher review is still open.

## 6. Rollout

Deploy the code first, then `bank:topup --production --sync-seed` (old code would draw new text items into normal
grammar forms). C-tests are not retired. Students invited before the switch who have not started keep C-tests.
