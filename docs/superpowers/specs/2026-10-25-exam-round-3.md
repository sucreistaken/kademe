# Exam round 3 (owner feedback 2026-10-25)

Owner request, overnight, deploy when done ("bittikten sonra canlıya al"). Everything applies to BOTH the
placement exam and the level verification exam (same candidate screens; check both templates).

## Owner points (ledger)

1. Pre-start warning: the "Hazırsınız / three short rules" step before the exam starts must clearly say
   "close every other application and tab; your screen, camera and voice are recorded and visible to the
   teacher". Make it impossible to miss (size, weight, contrast), not small pale pills.
2. During the exam the "screen / camera / audio recorded" indicator is small grey text top right. Move it next
   to the clock so it draws attention (the clock, Next and Mark are where the student looks).
3. Voice / background sound detected while solving ("Bu bölümde sesli konuşmayın"): today a thin 13px line under
   the header. Make that warning much bigger and obvious (non-blocking, never red, never auto-fail).
4. Text insertion ("Welcher Satz passt in welche Lücke?"): the A-G list plus a row of A-G buttons per gap is
   confusing, and the sticky option tray covers the gap rows (screenshot bug). New flow: click the gap [1] in the
   text, a dropdown opens, pick the sentence; letters already used drop out of (or sink to the bottom of) the other
   gaps' choices. Simple, few elements.
5. Same cleanup for the other matching screens: person <-> course/advert matching (cards were cramped, titles
   wrapped next to the letter disc) and the Ja / Nein / Text sagt dazu nichts statements.
6. Instructions and patterns (Aufgabenstellungen) must read like real ÖSD / telc / Goethe / TestDaF tasks.
7. Writing task screen: fix typography (weights, hierarchy, bullets), it looks messy.
8. Speaking screen UI: fix it (layout, recording state, buttons).
9. "How it works" tutorial animations must follow the new screens.
10. After the exam: an experience survey where the student gives a rating.
11. Bank: take more formats from the books (ÖSD, telc, TestDaF, Goethe model tests), comprehensive exam.
12. Grading: check again and again whether the AI grades writing and speaking correctly against fixed standards
    (CEFR descriptors, exam rating criteria); A0 must be a possible outcome.
